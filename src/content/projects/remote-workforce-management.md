Knowledge Transfer Partnership (KTP) project between **British Telecommunications PLC** and the **University of Essex** to develop remote workforce management solutions and embed knowledge of advanced computational intelligence, intelligent environments, and augmented reality.

## Project Details

- **Role**: KTP Associate
- **Partners**: British Telecommunications PLC (BT Labs, Adastral Park) & University of Essex
- **Project Identifier**: [KTP9985]
- **Focus**: Field operations, remote workforce scheduling, augmented reality field assistance, and computational intelligence decision systems.

## Key Outcomes & Recognition

The project delivered significant commercial and academic impact, resulting in multiple prestigious awards and publications:

- **Best Knowledge Transfer Project Associate Award** (Winner, 2016) — University of Essex Knowledge Transfer Partnerships Awards.
- **IEEE Computational Intelligence Society Outstanding Organisation Award** (2017) — Awarded to BT for innovations including the KTP field intelligence system.
- **Global Telecoms Business (GTB) Innovation Award** (2017) — In the Software & Applications category.
- **IET Innovation Awards Highly Commended** (2018) — In the Information Technology category.
- Multiple peer-reviewed conference and journal publications on Type-2 fuzzy logic and AR geolocation in field environments.
