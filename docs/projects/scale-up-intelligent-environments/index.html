Scale up Intelligent Environments

Jan 2011  –  to Jan 2016 Research
Intelligent Environments Mixed Reality Distributed Systems Internet of Things

A 5-year joint research collaboration between the University of Essex (UK) and King Abdulaziz University (KSA).

Its purpose was to investigate the Scaling-Up of Intelligent Environments from the small scale (single rooms), beyond four walls to the large-scale (complete campuses with multi-story large footprint buildings).
