About#

I am a Computer Scientist, Distinguished Engineer, and Research Technical Manager at BT Research Labs, as well as an Honorary Senior Lecturer at the University of Essex and a Senior Member of the IEEE (SMIEEE).

With over two decades of international experience bridging industrial R&D and academia, my work focuses on translating frontier technologies into scalable, strategic solutions. My research spans cyber-physical systems, digital twins, mixed reality (XR), and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on human-computer interaction and collaborative technologies.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science and an MSc in Advanced Web Engineering from the University of Essex, following a BEng in Information Technology from the Instituto Politécnico Nacional (Mexico). Over the course of my career, I have authored more than 100 peer-reviewed academic publications and hold multiple granted patents. My contributions have been recognised across the UK technology sector, including the TechWomen100 Award, alongside multiple nominations for the Computing Women in Tech Excellence Awards (Rising Star in 2018; Transformation Leader of the Year in 2025) and the ITP Woman in Technology Award (2025).

Alongside research, I actively contribute to academic governance and technology policy. I serve on as a Non-Executive Director of the Immersive Learning Research Network (iLRN) and the IEEE Education Society’s Technical Committee on Immersive Learning Environments. In addition, I contribute as an advisor to the Creative Science Foundation (CSf) and serve on the Advisory Group for the UK Digital Twin Hub (DT Hub), an initiative supported by Innovate UK / UKRI working in collaboration with the Department for Business and Trade (formerly BEIS). I also regularly co-organise international conferences, academic workshops, and special journal issues in these fields.
