Research collaboration investigating autonomous cognitive agent architectures for personal risk evaluation and decision support at the Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana (UAM), Mexico.

## Project Details

- **Role**: Co-Investigator
- **Institution**: Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana (UAM), Cuajimalpa, Mexico
- **Registration Year**: 2020
- **Duration**: 36 months
- **Project Identifier**: [DCCD.TI.PI-64](http://dccd.cua.uam.mx/repositorio/doc/pi/PI_Sistema_de_gestion_de_riesgo.pdf)
- **Official Portal**: [BDI Risk Management Website](http://bdi-dr.cua.uam.mx/)

## Focus & Objectives

The project focuses on developing cognitive models and intelligent agent systems capable of assessing individual risk factors across varying contexts, synthesizing data to provide proactive, personalized risk recommendations and decision support.
