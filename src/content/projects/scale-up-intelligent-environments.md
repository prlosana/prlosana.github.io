A 5-year joint international research collaboration between the **University of Essex** (UK) and **King Abdulaziz University** (KSA).

## Project Details

- **Role**: PhD Researcher
- **Institutions**: University of Essex (UK) and King Abdulaziz University (KSA)
- **Duration**: 5 years (2011 – 2016)
- **Core Vision**: Scaling intelligent environments beyond traditional small-scale smart rooms into large-scale interconnected intelligent spaces.

## Focus & Scope

Its purpose was to investigate the Scaling-Up of Intelligent Environments from the current small scale, beyond four walls to the large-scale — extending across complete university campuses with multi-story, large footprint buildings.

The research developed methodologies for:
- Connecting real and virtual intelligent inhabited environments across geographic boundaries.
- Scaling dual reality from individual single-user setups to large distributed collaborative groups.
- Integrating sensor/actuator networks with high-level spatial and computational models.
